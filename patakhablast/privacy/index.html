Patakha Blast: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Patakha Blast ("the game") is made by RashiNova (contact: rashinovasupport@gmail.com). This policy explains what data the game uses.

1. Data the game keeps on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, best score, daily gift streak), your settings (sound, music, vibration, language), the age group you choose (under 13, 13 to 17, 18 or older) and your ads choice are saved on your device. We do not run servers, we do not create accounts and we never receive this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it, unless you keep a copy with Play Games (section 2).

2. Optional Play Games save (18 or older only)

Players aged 18 or older can choose "Login with Play Games" (or "Play as Guest"). If you log in, the game uses Google Play Games Services to sign you in and keeps one saved game in your own Google Play Games account: your stars, levels, coins, daily gift streak and game statistics. Your settings, age group and ads choice are not copied. The copy is stored by Google under your Google account; we do not receive it, your name or your email. It is used only to bring your progress back after a reinstall or on a new phone. You can log out in Settings at any time; to delete the saved copy, remove the game's data in the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings (Play Games profile). Players under 18 never see this option. See Google's privacy policy.

3. Sharing

The Share button opens Android's own share menu with a picture made on your device (your level or score). It goes only to the app and people you pick; the game does not send or collect anything. Sharing is not offered to players under 13.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen, full-screen ads between levels or when you return to the game, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for a reward. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, app activity and diagnostic information. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • Players under 18 only get non-personalised, family-safe ads. For players under 13, ad requests are marked as child-directed and limited to content suitable for all ages.
  • Adults choose once whether ads may be personalised and can change this in Settings.
  • Where the law requires it (for example the EEA and UK), Google's consent form is shown before ads load, and "Privacy choices" in Settings lets you change your choice.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

5. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game asks for an age group only to choose the right kind of ads; the answer stays on the device.

6. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete; your data lives on your device, with Google for ads and, if you chose it, in your Play Games account (section 2). For questions or complaints, write to rashinovasupport@gmail.com. We answer within 15 days.

7. Changes

If the game starts handling data differently (for example online features or purchases), we will update this page and the date above before releasing that version.
