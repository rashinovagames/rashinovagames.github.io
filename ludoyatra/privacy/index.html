Ludo Yatra Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026 · App: Ludo Yatra (com.rashinova.jhoothludo) · Developer: RashiNova (an individual developer, India)

Ludo Yatra is a Ludo game with snakes and ladders and a classic mode. We collect as little as possible. This policy explains what the app uses, why, and your choices. It applies to the Android app and its online game server.

1. What stays on your phone only

These are stored on your device and are not sent to us:

  • Your age group (Under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 or older), which you choose once.
  • If you are 18 or older, your choice of personalised or general ads.
  • Game progress: Bharat Yatra stars, lives, skins, settings, language and match statistics.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data, unless your phone's own Android backup is switched on: then Android may save a copy of the game's data (progress, settings and the app's random device code) in your own Google account and put it back when you reinstall. We do not receive that backup; it is managed by Google and your phone settings. The app also keeps its random device code and a copy of your game progress (stars, coins, level, looks, lives, stats, settings, your age group answer and your nickname) in Google's Block Store (part of Google Play services on your phone), so that a new phone set up from your Google backup can get everything back by itself, without any sign-in. Google keeps it on your phone and, with your phone's Google backup on, end-to-end encrypted in your own Google account; we do not receive it. Google Play services removes this copy when you uninstall the app, so to get your progress back after uninstalling and reinstalling, players aged 18 or older can choose Login with Play Games (section 2), and everyone can use the Recovery code for the player ID. To remove it, use Settings › Delete my data and start fresh in the game: this deletes your online data on our server, the progress on your phone and this copy, and cannot be undone.

We do not collect your phone number, email, contacts, location, photos or files to play.

Our game server is hosted by Microsoft Azure in India (Central India region).

We use this data only to run the online features you choose to use (Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). Using online play is optional: offline modes need no data on our server, and you can delete your online data at any time.

3. Purchases

The app has no purchases yet. When available, in-app purchases (such as VIP or Remove Ads) will be processed by Google Play. We will receive confirmation that a purchase happened, but never your card, UPI or bank details. See the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising

The free app shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between matches (never during a turn), and optional videos you can choose to watch for a reward inside the game (an extra life, an extra daily spin or extra daily coins). Coins are only earned in the game: they cannot be bought, and they cannot be exchanged for money or prizes. Google (not RashiNova) collects the data used to show, measure and protect these ads, such as your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive this data.

  • If you are 18 or older, the app asks you once whether Google may personalise ads for you. You can change this at any time in Settings. If you say no, you get general (non-personalised) ads.
  • If you are under 18, the app never asks for personalised ads: it requests family-safe, non-personalised ads only, and for players under 13 it marks ad requests as directed to children.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads). See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Weekly leaderboard and gift card prizes

Ludo Yatra has no entry fees, no stakes and no real-money play.

The weekly leaderboard shows the week's top players, based on Yatra Points that our server works out from online matches you play yourself. Points cannot be bought, and nothing needs to be bought to take part. The leaderboard shows player IDs only (for example JL-48213), never names, and does not show points or ranks.

A player ID that cheats or abuses online play can be removed from the leaderboard and blocked. The block stays with that player ID until we lift it or the ID is deleted.

The app has no prizes yet. When available, the following applies to players aged 18 or older in India, under rewards rules that we will publish before prizes start:

  • Prizes go to an in-app reward wallet linked to your player ID. The wallet cannot be withdrawn as cash and cannot be topped up with money.
  • From Rs 10 you can ask for a Flipkart or Amazon Pay e-gift card. We buy the card and show its code inside the app, usually within 24 to 72 hours. We do not ask for your email, phone number, UPI or bank details for this.
  • Recent winners are listed in the app by player ID only (no names, amounts or ranks).
  • We keep a payout record (player ID, amount, gift card brand, date) for accounting.

6. Children

Children can play Ludo Yatra. The app asks for an age group on first launch and, for players under 18: requests non-personalised, family-safe ads only (and marks ad requests as directed to children for players under 13), never collects the ad-revenue estimate, does not track or profile them for advertising, does not allow prize claims, and does not offer Login with Play Games (only players aged 18 or older are offered it). Online play has no free-text chat, and abusive nicknames are replaced. If you are a parent and believe your child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with the services that run the app: Google (Play Store, AdMob and, only if you choose to log in, Google Play Games) and Microsoft Azure (server hosting). When prizes are available we buy gift cards ourselves (or through a gift card company); we do not share your personal data with them.

If you tap Share, your phone's own share menu opens and the result picture and text go to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp, Facebook or Instagram). We do not receive them, and that app's own privacy policy applies. The picture shows only your own result, not other players' names. Sharing and invites are not offered to players under 13.

Now and then, after a match you won, the app may show Google Play's own rating box (never to players under 13). Your rating and review go to Google Play under Google's terms; we do not learn whether or how you rated, and there is never a reward for rating.

8. Your rights and deleting your data

You can delete your player ID and everything our server keeps for it (weekly points, rewards, the ad-revenue estimate and, if you logged in with Play Games, the Play Games link and your saved-game copy) yourself in the app: Settings › Delete my online data. To delete everything at once (also your progress on the phone and its automatic save in Block Store), use Settings › Delete my data and start fresh. You can also email rashinovasupport@gmail.com with your player ID (shown in Play Online) to ask what we hold, to correct it or to delete it; we reply and delete within 30 days, including from backups. Records of gift cards already sent are kept for accounting as the law requires. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app (unless your phone's Android backup restores it; see section 1).

9. Security

The server never stores names or IP addresses. Game data travels only between your phone and our server and is encrypted on the way (DTLS); the app only connects to our own server, and your player ID can only be used from your own phone. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the data we hold to a minimum. If a data breach ever affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Changes and ending online features

We will update this page when the app changes and update the date at the top.

Online play, the weekly leaderboard and prizes may be paused, changed or stopped at any time. If we stop the leaderboard or prizes, we will say so in the app at least 30 days before, and you can still ask for the gift cards your wallet balance already covers during that time. Offline play (vs Computer, Bharat Yatra and Pass & Play) keeps working without our server.
